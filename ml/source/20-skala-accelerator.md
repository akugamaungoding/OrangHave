## Ringkasan

SKALA is Indonesia's first metrics-driven and growth-focused pre-seed accelerator program by Innovation Factory for early stage startups in Indonesia and Southeast Asia. The program leverages on the expertise and network of Innovation Factory and VC partners.

Innovation Factory is a community driven platform in Indonesia which enables key actors in the tech startup ecosystem to meet, collaborate and grow together since 2017, with BLOCK71 as one of its earliest projects. The organization also manages an early stage startup fund and establishes partnerships with other organizations and companies.

Program dirancang untuk membekali tim pendiri dengan pengetahuan startup fundamentals dan key business metrics melalui workshop terstruktur dan sesi networking.

## Overview of SKALA Accelerator

+1500 applications · +200 hours of workshops ran · +50 mentors · 9 startups funded

Contoh mentor & pembicara: Junerosano (Founder & CEO, Waste4Change), David Setyadi (Founder & CEO, EdenFarm), Irzan Raditya (Co-founder & CEO, Kata AI), Gary Khoeng (Partner, Vertex Ventures SE Asia & India), Aswin T. Utomo (Tokopedia), dan lainnya.
