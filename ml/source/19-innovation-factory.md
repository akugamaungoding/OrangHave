## Ringkasan

"Where you innovate, we empower, and the whole world connects."

Innovation Factory works alongside entrepreneurs, corporates, and ecosystem players to build capabilities, open access to markets and networks across Indonesia and the region, and sustain innovation through readiness, partnerships, and continuous support. As the operator of BLOCK71 Indonesia — our partnership with NUS Enterprise — we serve as the epicentre for early-stage tech innovators in Indonesia.

## Tiga pilar

- **Build** — Refining solutions and fostering talent through capacity-building and community development.
- **Access** — Opening market access and connecting innovators to the networks they need to scale.
- **Sustain** — Bridging long-term partnerships and investment to grow resilient, future-ready ventures.
