# NISAR Applications White Papers (Societal Benefits)

- **Source URL:** https://science.nasa.gov/mission/nisar/societal-benefits/
- **Priority for OrangHave / H.O.M.E.:** Utama
- **Relevance:** Justifikasi dampak sosial untuk pitch Space Apps; fokus fire, forest, timber/disturbance, floods.

## Deskripsi halaman

Dalam white paper ringkas (≈2 halaman), NASA merangkum aplikasi NISAR yang memberi manfaat sosial, diidentifikasi dari NISAR Applications Workshops 2014 & 2015 serta workshop area-spesifik berikutnya.

Workshop reports:
- 2014: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/missions/nisar/nisar-jpl/pdf/2014_NISAR_Applications_Workshop_Report1.pdf
- 2015: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/missions/nisar/nisar-jpl/pdf/2015_NISAR_Application_Workshop_Report_20160926.pdf
- Workshops index: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/nisar/workshops/
