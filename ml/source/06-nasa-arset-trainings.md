ARSET
Applied Remote Sensing Training Program
Build skills to use remote sensing data for a range of applications related to air quality, public health, agriculture, disasters, ecological conservation, water resource management, climate resilience, and wildland fires. From "How do satellites work?" to "How can I use satellite data to make a flood map?," there are trainings available for all levels of experience at no cost.

Not sure where to start? You can filter by theme, training level, and training type. Or browse our Online Resource Guide (PDF, 7.8 MB) to quickly see descriptions of our online trainings since 2015.
