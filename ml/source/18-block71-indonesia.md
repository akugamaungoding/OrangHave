## Ringkasan

NUSX's global commercialisation engine. Founders and leading companies building what the world needs most, side by side. B71 connects research, capital, pilots and markets across 11 locations in 6 countries, in Asia and North America.

**FIFTEEN YEARS OF PROOF:** 1,600+ startups supported since 2011 · 11 locations worldwide · 6 countries · 150 active in Singapore today · 15 years of company building.

Alumni & partners: home to Carousell, PatSnap and ShopBack; working with corporates including Microsoft and TISI.

## Program (B71 for Founders)

1. **Incubate** — Turn an idea into a company. Incubation at B71 exists for one job: to build ventures worth taking to market.
2. **Build** — Work in the same rooms as corporate partners and NUS research, turning pilots and problems into products.
3. **Accelerate** — Expand into new markets and verticals. B71's on-the-ground network across Asia and Silicon Valley opens commercial and investment opportunities.

**B71 for Corporates:** structured access to vetted NUS-affiliated startups, to solve real business problems and bring new technology to market.
